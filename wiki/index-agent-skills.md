- [Project Graveyard](project-graveyard.md) — Finds every side project you abandoned, tells you why each one died, and helps you finish the one worth going back to
- [First Reader](first-reader.md) — Simulates real readers going through your draft and reports where they lose interest, where they stop reading, and what they remember afterward, without…

- [Commit Archaeologist](commit-archaeologist.md) — Reconstructs why a file or code region exists from its introducing commit, later edits, co-changes, and intent clues
- [Dependency Doctor](dependency-doctor.md) — Checks a dependency manifest for standard-library pins, obsolete backports, unpinned entries, duplicate constraints, and yanked releases
- [Advisor Orchestrator Worker](advisor-orchestrator-worker.md) — Meta Loop with Claude Fable 5.1 as advisor, GPT-6 Astra as orchestrator, and Gemini 3.8 Flash as worker
- [Thinking Out Loud](thinking-out-loud.md) — Echoes a voice ramble back as a scannable brief, with the model's guesses quarantined and your reversals flagged
- [Self-Improving Agent Skills](self-improving-agent-skills.md) — Automatically optimize agent skills using Gemini and ADK
